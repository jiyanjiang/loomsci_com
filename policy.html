Content Policy

All content published on LoomSci is generated with the assistance of AI tools and reviewed by human editors. We strive for accuracy and fairness in every article we publish.

  • We cite original research papers and provide links to source material.
  • AI-generated content is clearly identified as such.
  • We do not publish content that misrepresents scientific findings.
  • Corrections are made promptly when errors are identified.

Privacy Policy

LoomSci respects your privacy. We collect minimal data necessary to operate the site:

We do not sell, share, or distribute personal information to third parties.
