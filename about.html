About LoomSci

LoomSci is a science communication platform dedicated to making cutting-edge research accessible to everyone. We believe that scientific knowledge should not be locked behind paywalls or buried in jargon — it belongs to all of us.

Our Mission

We translate complex scientific discoveries into clear, engaging narratives that anyone can understand. From quantum physics to artificial intelligence, from materials science to robotics — if it advances human knowledge, we want to tell that story.

AI-Assisted Communication

At LoomSci, AI tools assist our human editors — they don't replace them. Every article is researched, edited, and approved by our editorial team. AI helps us scale; humans ensure quality. Our editorial workflow includes AI-assisted scoring, drafting, and image generation, but all final creative and editorial decisions are made by human editors who review, select, and approve every piece before publication.

Our Community

LoomSci is part of the Know Science CN community, a growing network of science enthusiasts, researchers, and communicators who believe that science literacy is a public good.

Team

LoomSci is run by a small team of science communicators and editors. We are building a platform where AI tools and human judgment work together to bring scientific discoveries to a wider audience.
